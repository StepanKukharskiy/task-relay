Task Relay for ChatGPT · Effective 2 October 2026

Privacy policy

This policy covers the skills-only Task Relay ChatGPT plugin published by Stepan Kukharskiy. It does not describe Relay Desktop or a separately connected development MCP app.

Your conversations and files

The plugin supplies workflow instructions that run within ChatGPT. It works with context available in the current conversation and material you explicitly supply or select. The skills-only workflow has no Relay server, account store, project database or cloud synchronization. The publisher does not receive your chat context or saved work through a Relay endpoint.

ChatGPT processes your interaction and any files under OpenAI's applicable terms, privacy practices and your account settings. Downloaded files remain with you. Files saved or uploaded to ChatGPT remain in that environment; removing a file from a conversation or local folder is handled by that environment.

Website and support

The publisher's website is hosted by Railway. Hosting infrastructure may process traffic metadata and operational logs under Railway's privacy policy. No request-body logging or additional analytics is enabled by the publisher for this plugin. No hosting retention period is promised by this policy.

If you email support, the publisher receives your email address and the contents you choose to send. Support email is handled in Gmail under Google's practices. Messages are retained only as needed to handle the request; you can request deletion through the support email. Do not send passwords, access keys or unrelated private material.

Use and sharing

The publisher does not sell submitted support information, use it for advertising or use it to train models. No separate model or provider API is called by this skills-only plugin. ChatGPT, website hosting and email service providers operate under their own practices.

Contact and changes

Contact stepan.kukharskiy@gmail.com for privacy questions or support-email deletion requests. Manage ChatGPT files and account data through ChatGPT, and downloaded files on your device. Changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

Keep the useful part.

Turn selected text into reusable Skills and work you can pick up again.

Two simple actions

Turn into Skill keeps a reusable guide for similar tasks, in standard SKILL.md format. Save work keeps a structured summary of this particular work, the Relay way.

  1. Select useful text on a webpage and open Task Relay from Chrome’s toolbar.
  2. Choose Turn into Skill or Save work. With optional ChatGPT autofill approved, Relay opens ChatGPT with the request ready.
  3. Review and send that message yourself, then download the result. Use Edit to adjust a proposed result.

Keep your exported files. In another chat, supply those files to your AI; the original conversation is not required. Options → Open files can prepare continuation context for you to copy.

You choose what to keep

No Relay account, cloud storage, Relay Desktop or separate model setup is required. Relay does not monitor browsing or read your browsing-history database. It captures material only after your explicit action.

The direct draft-and-result handoff supports ordinary ChatGPT conversations. Other AI sites use the manual copy/paste route. ChatGPT or another AI account and its own terms may apply. The extension never clicks Send.

Optional Desktop connection

Options → Connect Relay Desktop is an advanced local-library mode. It requires separate native-bridge registration and an optional Chrome permission. The portable flow works without it.
